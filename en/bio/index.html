Biography

Alexander Shurubkin's path is unique: from preparing cosmonauts for spacewalks to educating professional diving instructors. Both careers share one essence: teaching people to work underwater confidently and safely.

1979
Joined the Gagarin Cosmonaut Training Center (Star City).
1979 — 1997
Hydro laboratory of the Cosmonaut Training Center: senior test engineer, senior researcher, laboratory head. Trained more than 60 cosmonauts and astronauts, including NASA crews, for extravehicular activity in simulated weightlessness.
1990s
Transition to recreational diving. PADI certification, instructor at Moscow's Aquanaut club — Russia's first PADI 5★ CDC center.
2000s
PADI Course Director. Regular IDC programs in Moscow, Hurghada and Sharm El Sheikh. The first PADI Platinum Course Director in Russia.
Today
Seven-time PADI Platinum Course Director. More than 300 instructors trained and over 1000 certifications issued. Seminar photo archive maintained since 2007.

Alexander Shurubkin spent almost two decades at the hydro laboratory of the Gagarin Cosmonaut Training Center, rising from senior test engineer to laboratory head. Under his guidance and with his direct participation, dozens of cosmonauts and astronauts — including international program participants and NASA crews — prepared for spacewalks.

In the 1990s this experience found its continuation in recreational diving: after certifying with PADI, Alexander became an instructor and later a Course Director — the highest professional teaching rank in the system, responsible for educating new instructors. He became the first PADI Platinum Course Director in Russia and confirmed the Platinum status seven times.

Today Alexander runs IDC (Instructor Development Course) programs and professional seminars in Russia and Egypt. His graduates — more than three hundred instructors — work in dive centers around the world.

Courses & IDC Programs

Professional PADI instructor training led by a Platinum Course Director: IDC seminars in Moscow and the Moscow region (Aquanaut club — Russia's first PADI 5★ CDC, with a site in Shatura) and travelling seminars in Egypt — Hurghada and Sharm El Sheikh.

The PADI Pro pathway

Open Water→Advanced→Rescue→Divemaster→OWSI / IDC→MSDT→Master Instructor→Course Director

IDC Seminar — Instructor Development Course

Complete PADI instructor (OWSI) training: system theory and standards, teaching methodology for classroom, pool and open water, IE exam preparation. The seminar runs about 10 days, with IE exams on the following weekend.

IDC Staff Instructor

Program for instructors who want to take part in training new instructors: IDC auditing, candidate evaluation methodology, working on a Course Director's team.

From beginner to instructor

The complete path into the profession from zero: consistent training from Open Water Diver to professional ranks under unified methodological guidance.

Professional seminars

Regular seminars on PADI standards, program updates and teaching methodology. The seminar photo archive goes back to 2007.

The training builds on methodology proven in the two most demanding schools of underwater work: the space program and the PADI professional system. Candidates receive not just exam preparation but real teaching skills. The graduates’ reputation speaks for itself: “Divemasters trained by Shurubkin pass the instructor exam with high scores — they don’t fail.”

Practical details. Registration for an IDC seminar closes two months before it starts. Indicative IDC participation cost is from $600 (per the shurubkin.ru price list; confirm the current price and terms when applying — they depend on the venue and program).

Current dates are in the seminar schedule. To apply, get in touch via any channel on the contacts page.

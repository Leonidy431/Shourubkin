Star City Hydro Laboratory

The hydro laboratory of the Gagarin Cosmonaut Training Center is a unique facility where cosmonauts rehearse spacewalk operations in simulated weightlessness underwater.

The hydro laboratory was built in Star City in 1980 specifically to prepare cosmonauts for extravehicular activity. Full-scale mockups of orbital station modules sit on a movable platform inside a huge pool, and a cosmonaut in a spacesuit balanced to neutral buoyancy spends hours rehearsing the operations to be performed in open space.

At the hydro laboratory Alexander Shurubkin rose from senior test engineer to laboratory head. Dozens of cosmonauts and astronauts, including international program crews and NASA astronauts, prepared for spacewalks under his guidance and with his participation.

Working in simulated weightlessness demands the highest standards of safety, methodology and precision: instructor-divers accompany a suited cosmonaut through hours-long training sessions, securing every move. This very experience became the foundation of the professional diver and instructor training methodology that Alexander develops today as a PADI Course Director: a training system fit for a space program can certainly produce the finest diving instructors.

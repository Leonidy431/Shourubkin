Star City Hydro Laboratory

The hydro laboratory of the Gagarin Cosmonaut Training Center is a unique facility where cosmonauts rehearse spacewalk operations in simulated weightlessness underwater.

At the hydro laboratory Alexander Shurubkin rose from senior test engineer to laboratory head. Dozens of cosmonauts and astronauts, including international program crews, prepared for spacewalks under his guidance and with his participation.

Working in simulated weightlessness demands the highest standards of safety, methodology and precision: instructor-divers accompany a suited cosmonaut through hours-long training sessions imitating a spacewalk.

This very experience became the foundation of the professional diver and instructor training methodology that Alexander develops today as a PADI Course Director.

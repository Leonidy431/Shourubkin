On 4 October 2015, an instructor seminar at the Aquanaut club marked a special milestone: the **five hundredth** PADI OWSI instructor trained by Alexander Shurubkin over his Course Director career was certified. His name is Maxim Astakhov.

Every candidate of that class passed the exams — a tradition Alexander's graduates uphold year after year.

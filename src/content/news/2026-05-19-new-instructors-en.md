The Aquanaut club congratulates its new PADI instructors! Six candidates from three cities and two countries completed their training and passed the IE instructor exams held in Egypt and Thailand:

- Alexander Sushilnikov (Kazakhstan)
- Alexey Puzanov and Sergey Avdeev (Yaroslavl)
- Sergey Erman, Natalia Kazakova and Olga Kuzmina (Moscow)

Congratulations to the graduates on earning the PADI OWSI professional rating — fair winds in your teaching careers!

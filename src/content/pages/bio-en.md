Alexander Shurubkin spent eighteen years — from 1979 to 1997 — at the hydro laboratory of the Gagarin Cosmonaut Training Center, rising from senior test engineer to head of the research and testing laboratory. Under his guidance and with his direct participation, more than sixty cosmonauts and astronauts — including European program participants and NASA crews — prepared for spacewalks in simulated weightlessness. He holds the academic rank of senior researcher and the military rank of lieutenant colonel.

The hydro laboratory itself led him to PADI diving: instructors of Moscow's Aquanaut club, who ran diving courses there, told him about the first PADI instructor seminar in Russian history, held at Aquanaut in July 1996. On 16 July 1997 Alexander became a PADI instructor, and in 2002 — a Course Director.

Since 2003 he has worked at the Aquanaut club — Russia's first PADI dive center and the first holder of the top PADI 5★ Career Development Centre rating. His record also includes Red Sea College in Sharm El Sheikh, named the world's best PADI dive center in 2006.

In 2006 PADI introduced its highest distinction for Course Directors — the Platinum title. Alexander became its first and only holder in Russia — of roughly 1,100 active Course Directors worldwide, only 62 then held it — and confirmed the Platinum status seven times. His graduates number more than five hundred instructors; the milestone 500th, Maxim Astakhov, was certified on 4 October 2015 at an Aquanaut seminar. His total instructor-level certifications exceed one thousand.

At the club, his graduates' reputation is summed up simply: "Divemasters trained by Shurubkin pass the instructor exam with high scores — they don't fail."
